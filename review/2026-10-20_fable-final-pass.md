# Final pass on deck v8 (built from v7 plus the reassessment)

Prepared 2026-10-20, during the v8 build. Scope: everything the reassessment (`2026-10-20_fable-reassessment.md`, section 2 revisions 1-7, section 4 talk track) does not already cover. Inputs: the brief, v7 slide text and speaker notes, the v7 talk track, rendered slides 9 and 13. Rules kept: no new Nu data, no new slides, 15 presented slides, about 24 minutes, sentence case, placeholders stay off slides.

Old text is copied exactly from v7 so it can be found and replaced. Where a revision in the reassessment touches the same slide, apply that first; these edits sit beside it.

## Ranked punch list

**1. Slide 9. The Board rhythm reads as a one-off countdown; the brief asks for a "seamless Board management rhythm" and "pre-Board alignment routines", and the ask on slide 2 is "the Board calendar" without saying what that is.**
Old (title): `The Board book goes out 7 days before the meeting, after a lock at 16 days`
New (title): `Every quarter: the book goes out 7 days before the Board, after a lock at 16 days`
Old (lead line): `Why the book is late: no locked outline, no standard data tables, and units building rival decks. The fix starts today, with the Corporate Secretary.`
New (lead line): `Why the book is late: no locked outline, no standard data tables, and units building rival decks. The fix starts today, with the Corporate Secretary, and the same countdown then runs for every Board from the Secretary's calendar. That calendar is the one the ask approves.`
Why: turns the slide from this Board's rescue into the standing routine the panel is scoring, and defines the second item in the ask. Title stays under 14 words.

**2. Slide 2. The D3 and D2 tiles will contradict the revised slides 7, 11 and 12 once revisions 2, 3 and 6 land.**
Old (D3 tile): `Four gates in 120 days Tax, grant terms, officers and lease, each with an owner, an exit test and a walk-away. Licensed executives sign; I run the pod.`
New (D3 tile): `Four decision gates in 120 days Can we, what may we offer, who may bind Nu, do we commit: each the CFO's call on a pack I assemble. Licensed executives sign; I run the pod.`
Old (D2 tile): `One capital ranking Both executives' positions go through the same test. The plan locks 16 days before the Board; the book ships 7 days before.`
New (D2 tile): `One test, one decision owner Both executives' positions go into the memo; the CEO decides at T-16; the dissent is recorded. The book ships 7 days before the Board.`
Why: the opening slide must say what slides 7 and 11 now say, or the panel hears two versions of the same mechanism eight minutes apart.

**3. Slide 2. The D4 tile uses the old framing; the new line is on slides 12 and 14 but not where the panel first reads it.**
Old (D4 tile): `Rigor inside the business I work in the numbers with the GMs. The CFO gets the trade-offs and makes the call.`
New (D4 tile): `Rigor inside the business I work in the numbers with the GMs. Experts own their judgment, I own the process, the CFO owns the decision and its residual risk.`
Why: makes the posture line the deck's refrain from slide 2 on, so it is remembered rather than noticed.

**4. Slides 15 and 25. A stop rule measured against the FY26 guide at a date in FY27 is the kind of detail a CFO reads twice.**
Old (slide 15, stop rules): `Efficiency ratio above the FY26 guide at Q1 close: the second release is held for every unit.`
New: `Efficiency ratio above the guide in force at Q1 close: the second release is held for every unit.`
Old (slide 25, stop rules row): `Efficiency ratio above the FY26 guide at Q1 close: the second release is held for every unit`
New: `Efficiency ratio above the guide in force at Q1 close: the second release is held for every unit`
Also on slide 25, H2 reserve row, old: `inside the ~20% FY26 guide` new: `inside the guide in force`.
Why: Q1 close is 31 March 2027; by then Nu will have guided FY27. The rule should survive the change of year without an edit. Spoken line on slide 15 changes to "If the efficiency ratio runs above guidance at Q1 close".

**5. Slide 13. The title is a topic, not an assertion, and the footer tells the pre-reader that the evidence is not in the document.**
Old (title): `How I balance strategic influence with hands-on rigor`
New (title): `A few clear rules, published early, and nothing more: three I hold myself to`
Old (footer): `Fair process: Kim and Mauborgne (HBR). Examples from my own record follow in the talk.`
New (footer): `Fair process: Kim and Mauborgne (HBR).`
Why: the deck is sent to Renata Alves 24 hours before the session and is read without the speaker. The new title is the lesson the talk track already states on this slide ("a few clear rules, published early, and nothing more"), so no new claim is made. The slide has empty space; nothing else needs to move. The talk is unchanged.

**6. Slide 4 footer. Two revenue bases sit in one footer and only one of them produces 15 bps.**
Old: `Sources: Nu Q2'26 release and 6-K (opex $806.2M; net revenue ~$4.1B), Q2'26 call, Form 20-F. Calculations: revenue ~$23.5B annualized managerial; opex ~$3.2B annualized; efficiency ratio on Nu's denominator (NII plus fees).`
New: `Sources: Nu Q2'26 release and 6-K (opex $806.2M; net revenue ~$4.1B a quarter), Q2'26 call, Form 20-F. Calculations: the 15 bps is on managerial revenue, ~$23.5B annualized; the 1.1% is on opex, ~$3.2B annualized; the efficiency-ratio points are on Nu's denominator (NII plus fees).`
Why: $4.1B a quarter annualizes to about $16B, not $23.5B; a CFO will do that sum in their head. Both figures are already in the source ledger (`2026-10-20_v7_talk-track-and-QA.md`, section 5), so this labels, it does not add. If Opus cannot confirm the $23.5B managerial figure in backup D, drop the bullet `About 15 bps of annualized revenue.` from the gap block instead and keep the other two.

**7. Slide 15. "Board by 17 Nov" reads as a deadline for a date that is fixed.**

New (title): `Next 90 days: the Board on 17 Nov, the first release on 1 January, and the ask`

Why: the notes are the candidate's prompt on the day; a stale prompt is how an old title gets spoken over a new slide.

**9. Slide 12. One sentence still says "I assemble the gate pack and recommend" in the signing block's neighbour after revision 3 rewrites the leadership block; check the risk card too.**

## What is still missing

Nothing a panel would mark down hard. The four deliverables each have an assessment, a recommendation, data with its class, risks with owners and a named decision owner; assumptions are separated from facts as the brief asks; the ask is measurable at 90 days. The one soft spot a CFO or search panel may still probe is that the deck's leadership evidence lives in bracketed story slots in the talk track and nowhere in the pre-read (slide 13 after item 5 is honest about this rather than silent). The cheapest fix is not on a slide: the candidate writes their three 40-word examples for slide 13 and the one for slide 8 tonight, in their own words, and rehearses them once with the clock, as the talk track already instructs. A second, smaller gap: slide 7 shows no return or cost-of-equity figures by design, and Q25 covers it; make sure the spoken line "I have not guessed it" stays in, because it is the sentence that turns the blank into a strength.
